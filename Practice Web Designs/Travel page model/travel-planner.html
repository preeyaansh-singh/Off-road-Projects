Smart planning tips

Book the big things first

Flights, popular restaurants and timed-entry attractions fill up fastest. Lock those in, then fill the gaps with flexible plans.

Leave room to wander

Aim for two or three anchors a day rather than a packed schedule. The best memories often come from unplanned detours.

Travel in shoulder season

Spring and autumn shoulders often bring milder weather, thinner crowds and noticeably lower prices than peak months.

Keep digital and paper backups

Save copies of tickets, bookings and your passport offline, and keep a printed set separate from your main bag.
